Night-by-night data and methods

Oura estimates sleep from sensors and can mistake quiet wakefulness for sleep. A gap between separate recorded sleep sessions is unknown. Summary awake totals and stage-derived totals can differ. HR and HRV samples and movement are retained privately in the local database; select a night to view their curves.
